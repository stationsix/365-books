# Batch 1: Strategy (theme 4), v02

Status: **draft for Joe's review**. v02 adds the persona wildcard round: 19 nominees, 2 became picks, 2 alternates and 10 routed to other themes. In total: 85 candidates scored, 30 picks, 5 alternates.

## How to review
Mark each pick **approve**, **veto** or **swap**. Anything you don't mark counts as approved.

## Method
- **Final score = average of the rubric score** (weighted 1 to 5 criteria from the brief) **and the panel score** (mean of six persona scores, 1 to 5).
- **The long list comes from** five public lists, Claude's own nominations from the canon, and 3 to 5 wildcards from each persona.
- **Debate decides** books near the cutoff, author caps, overlaps, theme placement and balance. Each book appears in one theme only.
- **Honesty note:** the personas and all scores are Claude's judgment, not data. The verification step later checks that each book's content can be confirmed from outside sources.

Sources: SF = [Shortform, 100 Best Business Strategy Books](https://shortform.com/best-books/genre/best-business-strategy-books-of-all-time) · WM = [WallStreetMojo, Top Strategy Books](https://wallstreetmojo.com/top-best-strategy-books) · TB = [TeamBuilding.com, Business Strategy Books](https://teambuilding.com/blog/business-strategy-books) · BI = [Business Initiative, Strategy Reading List](https://www.businessinitiative.org/business-tips/strategy-reading-list-curated-articles-books-busy-founders/) · AS = [AmberStudent, Best Strategy Books](https://amberstudent.com/blog/post/best-strategy-books)

## Wildcard debates
1. ***Monetizing Innovation* in, *Strategy Rules* out.** The Operator argued that pricing is where strategy turns into money, and nothing else on the list covers it. The Professor agreed that value capture is the missing half of 7 Powers. *Strategy Rules* (built from cases on Gates, Grove and Jobs) overlapped *Only the Paranoid Survive* the most.
2. ***The Innovation Stack*: Strategy or Entrepreneurship?** The Modernist said it's about building an advantage nobody can copy, which makes it a strategy book. The Operator agreed. It replaces *Co-opetition*, whose game-theory ideas are better covered in the Decisions theme.
3. ***The Three Rules* vs. *Hidden Champions*.** The Skeptic argued for *The Three Rules*, the most statistically careful book in the batch. The Professor and the Operator argued *Hidden Champions* brings the one non-US, mid-size-company view on the list. The vote went 4 to 2 for *Hidden Champions*, which shows the debate can overrule the scores. *The Three Rules* becomes an alternate.
4. ***This Is Strategy* vs. *The Strategist*.** The Reader's Advocate argued for Godin's readability and reach. The Professor and the Skeptic called it aphorisms rather than a method. The balance check decided it: *The Strategist* stays, and Godin becomes an alternate.
5. ***The Infinite Game* placement.** The Mindset Coach wanted it here. The panel agreed it's mostly about purpose and mindset rather than competitive strategy, so it moves to Mindset for Builders along with Carse's *Finite and Infinite Games*. That batch decides between them.
6. **The Modernist's AI picks** (*Co-Intelligence*, *Chip War*, *Working in Public*) and the Professor's *Lead and Disrupt* scored well but belong to Innovation and Technology, so they're routed there with their scores carried over.

## Round 1 debates (from v01)
1. ***Zero to One* placed in Strategy**, because Thiel's monopoly thesis is a strategy argument. That leaves room in Entrepreneurship.
2. **Both Porter originals are in and Magretta's *Understanding Michael Porter* is out**: a summary of a summary adds nothing on a summary site.
3. ***The Crux* is an alternate**, because its core is already in *Good Strategy Bad Strategy*.
4. **One McGrath:** *The End of Competitive Advantage* is the pick and *Seeing Around Corners* is the alternate.
5. ***My Years with General Motors* lifted from 39th**: it's the founding text on managing through decentralization, it fills the era gap, and Sloan's split between central policy and decentralized operations is a live question again with AI agents.
6. **Mintzberg's *The Rise and Fall of Strategic Planning* lifted from 53rd**, because a list full of planning frameworks needs its sharpest critic. The vote was 4 to 2.
7. ***Wardley Maps* is in.** Joe approved nontraditional books.
8. ***The Halo Effect* is in.** It takes apart the research method behind many classic business books, which suits the transparency angle.
9. ***In Search of Excellence* is cut**, because its claims didn't hold up.
10. ***Reshuffle* (2025) is an alternate**, to revisit in 2027.
