## Process notes for future batches
- Audiobook companion PDFs that list a book's figures and endnotes are strong evidence of which examples a book really uses.
- Examples from the authors' own HBR or journal articles get attached to the book; check them against the book's figures or chapter excerpts.
- A definition quoted in a secondary article may be that writer's paraphrase; trace it to the author's own text. A critic's line can also be the author's own phrase.
- Executives' names get added to company cases that only name the company.
- A critique only Wikipedia attributes should be swapped for a review that can be read directly. Label the drafter's own critiques as house caveats from the start.
