## Process notes for future batches
- Strategy Beyond the Hockey Stick has no critical trade or academic review that the reviewer could find; the summary says so rather than inflating vendor blogs into critiques.
- McKinsey Quarterly and HBR articles adapted from a book are a strong second source, but quotes from them must be credited to the article, not the book.
- Check that a critique attributed to a reviewer is the reviewer's own view: one McKinsey line was the authors quoting Collis and Montgomery, and one "caveat" was really praise.
