## Confirmed and worth noting
- Playing to Win: Harvard Business Review Press, 2013. Lafley CEO 2000 to 2009, returned May 2013 (P&G SEC filing). The five choices, the strategy logic flow (industry structure, customer value, relative position, competitive reaction), P&G's five core capabilities, the Gillette example and the seven-step "what would have to be true" process all match the book.
- Zero to One: Crown, September 2014. Grew from Masters' notes on Thiel's Stanford CS183 class (spring 2012). Both quotes verbatim. Four monopoly traits, last-mover idea, four outlooks on the future, the power law rule and all seven questions match.
- Only the Paranoid Survive: first edition subtitle "How to Exploit the Crisis Points That Challenge Every Company and Career", Currency Doubleday, 1996. Moore exchange (mid-1985) quoted as in the book; memory exit took about three years. Pentium facts: replace-on-request December 20, 1994; $475 million charge January 1995. Section headings (Helpful Cassandras, Strategic Dissonance, Let Chaos Reign, Rein in Chaos, Traversing the Valley of Death) are real.
