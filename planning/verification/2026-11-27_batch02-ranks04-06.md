## Process notes for future batches
- Commercial summary sites (SuperSummary and similar) are not enough on their own: confirm from publisher previews, the author's own articles or a named reviewer, or cut the example.
- Drafters again quoted more than two passages from sources; reviewers paraphrased the extras. Count quotes before handing a draft to review.
- Prediction Machines and Power and Prediction share authors; the review checked the two summaries do not substantially overlap.
